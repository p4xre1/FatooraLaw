================================================================================
 PART 0 — WHAT THIS PRODUCT IS
================================================================================
ONE repository contains THREE applications (they share one bundle and one Router):

  A. PUBLIC MARKETING SITE
     /  /features  /assistant  /about  /benefits  /pricing  /blog  /faq
     /contact  /privacy  /terms  /cookies   — each also at /ar/... and /en/...
     Plus /blog/<slug> article pages, published from the CMS.
     Fully prerendered to static HTML (one document per page per language).

  B. SIGNED-IN BUSINESS APPLICATION (the dashboard)
     13 modules: /dashboard /projects /contacts /procurement /inventory
     /calendar /risk /tools /legal /reports /team /audit /settings

  C. ADMIN CMS
     /admin/* — 18 routes, split into two separate roles:
     platform operators (subscriptions, traffic, security, notifications,
     system rules, support, backups) and content editors (website, pages,
     blog, monitor, content improvement, contact inbox).

TECHNOLOGY (package.json)
  React 19.3, Vite 8, TypeScript 5.7, Tailwind 4 (via @tailwindcss/vite),
  react-router 8 (only for /admin), a small custom router for the main app
  (src/lib/router.ts), Zustand 5 for state, Supabase JS v2 (auth + Postgres),
  lucide-react icons, self-hosted fonts (Sora, JetBrains Mono, Tajawal),
  Vitest + Testing Library (47 test files), Wrangler for Cloudflare Pages.

  The product is described in its own README as a prototype: business records
  live in the browser's localStorage, not in a production ERP database.

 PART 3 — THE PUBLIC WEBSITE, PART BY PART
================================================================================
All of it lives in src/marketing/Landing.tsx (single file, ~1660 lines) plus
src/marketing/landing.css and src/marketing/references.tsx.
The component reads its PAGE and its LANGUAGE from the URL:
/ar/pricing is the Arabic pricing page, /pricing the French one.

3.1 GLOBAL SHELL — every public page renders these four parts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 HEADER (function Header)
   - logo linking to / (per language)
   - desktop navigation: Features (a "mega menu" panel listing the feature
     cards and linking straight to the preview widgets), then Assistant,
     Benefits, Pricing, Blog, FAQ, About, Contact
   - the menu is keyboard driven: Escape closes it, a click outside closes it,
     focus returns to the button (refs + document listeners)
   - mobile: a hamburger that opens a full-screen drawer; a backdrop button
     labelled "Fermer la navigation" closes it
   - LanguageSwitcher — a real navigation, not a re-render: choosing a
     language navigates to /ar/... or /en/... so the URL, the back button and
     shares all keep the language
   - ThemeToggle — light/dark switch, applied through data-theme on the
     landing root, aria-label "Basculer le thème"
   - "Enter" buttons (sign up / sign in) -> onEnter -> navigate to /login

 ANNOUNCEMENT STRIP (function AnnouncementStrip)
   Renders at most one active announcement published in the CMS
   (/admin/website -> Announcements), with a dismiss button. Expired
   announcements (ends_at in the past) are skipped.

 FOOTER (function Footer)
   Brand block, navigation columns, legal links (Privacy / Terms / Cookies),
   contact details read from .figma/make/site.json through src/lib/contact.ts,
   and the sign-in entry point.

 COOKIE BANNER (function CookieBanner)
   Bottom dialog, "Accept" / "Decline". The choice is stored under the
   localStorage key fatorati-cookie-consent and broadcast as the
   fatorati:cookie-consent event, which is what switches analytics on or off
   (see 3.8). It is dismissible only by choosing.

3.2 HOME PAGE SECTIONS — in the order they appear on /
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1) HERO (function Hero)
    Headline + subtitle from i18n; call-to-action buttons; the hero can be
    overridden by CMS content (headline, subheadline, CTA, hero image) when a
    published row exists for the locale.
 2) DASHBOARD PREVIEW MOCKUP (function DashboardPreview)
    A static, illustrative picture of the product (cards + a trend chart), so
    visitors see the tool before signing up. It is a mock-up, not live data.
 3) FEATURES (function Features)
    - the three built-in feature cards: Calendar preview, Risk preview,
      Tools preview — each embeds a live mini-widget (see 3.5)
    - CMS-managed feature cards (function CmsFeatureCard) published per locale
      from /admin/website -> Features
 4) ASSISTANT (function Assistant)
    Describes the Fatorati Assistant: a panel that serves Moroccan legal rules
    and ready-to-use contract templates (examples shown: "Contrat de prestation
    — art. 723 DOC", "Clause de confidentialité (Loi 09-08)", "Facture
    conforme ICE + TVA 20 %"). Has its own page: /assistant.
 5) ABOUT (function AboutSection) — the project's positioning and audience.
 6) BENEFITS (function BenefitsSection) — four benefits (save time, stay
    compliant, pilot from your pocket, work as a team).
 7) PRICING (function PricingSection)
    - three plans with prices read from the same copy the JSON-LD uses:
      Essentiel 0 MAD, Pro 79 MAD / month, Équipe 250 MAD / month
    - choosing a plan leads to sign-up (no payment provider is integrated)
 8) ONE-TIME PRODUCTS (function OneTimeProductsSection)
    Products published in the CMS with a price and an EXTERNAL purchase URL.
    Clicking records a "purchase_click" event — explicitly labelled as a click
    out, not a confirmed payment.
 9) RELEASES + DOWNLOADS (function DownloadReleaseSection)
    Version notes published in the CMS and per-platform download links.
    A click records a "download_click" event = a click, not a completed
    download.
10) BLOG (function BlogSection)
    Cards of the latest published articles (title, excerpt, image); on /blog it
    lists everything; each card links to /blog/<slug>.
11) FAQ (function FaqSection)
    Accordion of questions; the same questions feed the FAQPage structured data.
12) CONTACT (function ContactSection) — email / phone / city from
    src/lib/contact.ts (a placeholder phone number is never published).
13) SUPPORT REQUEST FORM (function SupportRequestForm, /contact page)
    Name, email, category (contact / bug / feedback / feature), subject and
    message; writes a row into the Supabase table cms_support_requests, which
    platform operators read in /admin/support.
14) LEGAL SECTION (function LegalSection)
    Privacy, Terms and Cookies texts; on the home page the section is shown
    with no tab active, on /privacy /terms /cookies it opens the right one.
15) REFERENCE SECTION (src/marketing/references.tsx)
    A table of official Moroccan figures with their sources (VAT 20 %; CGI
    article 208 penalties 20 % / 10 %+5 %; Law 09-08 published 18 February
    2009, BO n° 5714; the plan prices) and a Q&A block. Present on every
    public page.

3.3 THE 11 STANDALONE PUBLIC PAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each non-home page renders a page-specific lead (function PageIntro) plus the
sections marked with it in the render tree:
  /features   -> full Features section
  /assistant  -> the Assistant section + entry buttons
  /about      -> About section
  /benefits   -> Benefits section
  /pricing    -> Pricing + one-time products + releases/downloads
  /blog       -> blog index (all published articles)
  /faq        -> FAQ
  /contact    -> contact details + support request form
  /privacy    -> legal section, privacy tab
  /terms      -> legal section, terms tab
  /cookies    -> legal section, cookies tab
A CMS-managed page (table cms_pages) can replace the built-in content of its
slug entirely: if the CMS publishes page X for the requested locale, the page
renders the CMS body (component ManagedPage) instead of the built-in sections.

3.4 BLOG ARTICLE PAGES  (/blog/<slug>, /ar/blog/<slug>, /en/blog/<slug>)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Component BlogArticlePage / BlogArticleView (same file).
  - reads the article from Supabase (published only) or, at build time, from
    the prerendered payload so the HTML is complete before JavaScript runs
  - renders title, author, date, cover image with alt text, the body split into
    paragraphs, and a language switcher between the article's translations
  - emits its own canonical URL, hreflang alternates for the languages that
    actually have a translation, and BlogPosting structured data
  - articles that do not exist in a requested language are not emitted as URLs
  - "Enter" CTA -> sign in

3.5 INTERACTIVE WIDGETS (embedded in the features cards)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CalendarWidget   a month grid with 7 pre-set events (renewals, payments,
                   legal deadlines) and a selected-day panel. Illustrative.
  RiskWidget       a list of risk rows (TVA, corporate tax, contracts,
                   insurance, registry, social, privacy) with High/Medium/OK
                   levels. Illustrative.
  SubscriptionWidget  a mini table of software subscriptions with monthly cost
                   and status. Illustrative.
  TrendChart       an inline SVG spend trend with a hover crosshair. The
                   numbers are sample data, labelled as an example in the copy.
All four are marketing illustrations; the real screens are in the dashboard.

3.6 LANGUAGE, THEME AND TEXT-DIRECTION BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - src/lib/locales.ts is the locale table: fr (fr-MA, prefix ""), ar (ar-MA,
    prefix /ar, RTL), en (en, prefix /en). It is the only place that knows an
    hreflang code.
  - <html lang dir> is set from the URL while a public page is mounted and
    restored when leaving, so Arabic is genuinely RTL for screen readers and
    crawlers.
  - Legacy anchors: an old /#pricing bookmark is rewritten client-side to the
    real /pricing URL (the fragment is stripped), and Supabase's
    #access_token=... recovery hash is deliberately left untouched.
  - Theme (light/dark) is landing-scoped state, applied as data-theme.

3.7 SEO / GEO / AIO LAYER — everything published for machines
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  src/lib/seo.ts owns the copy: PAGE_SEO (title/description per page),
  PAGE_COPY (per page per language), canonicalUrl(), hreflangAlternates(),
  buildSitemapXml(), buildLlmsTxt().
  src/marketing/Landing.tsx::buildJsonLd() builds per-page structured data
  (Organization, WebSite, Service/Product with offers, FAQPage, AboutPage,
  ItemList, BlogPosting on articles).
  scripts/prerender.mjs writes, at build time:
    - one HTML document per public route per language, each with its <title>,
      meta description, canonical, hreflang alternates, OpenGraph and JSON-LD
      already in the HTML (no JavaScript needed to be indexable)
    - dist/sitemap.xml — every localized URL with xhtml:link alternates and
      x-default, plus published blog articles
    - dist/llms.txt — a machine summary fed by the same route list
    - dist/_redirects — the explicit rewrite table described in PART 2
  Existing static machine files: robots.txt (disallows /admin/ and explicitly
  allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot, CCBot,
  etc.), ai.txt (AI guidance), ai-sitemap.xml,
  .well-known/ai-plugin.json and .well-known/openapi.json, security.txt,
  plus llms.txt (a machine index of routes and security context) and
  llms-full.txt (the fuller specification).
  public/_headers (CSP + X-Frame-Options: DENY + HSTS + Referrer-Policy +
  Permissions-Policy + X-Robots-Tag: noindex on /admin/*, immutable caching on
  /assets/*, Content-Language per language tree).
  Private screens are triple-protected from indexing: robots.txt, the
  X-Robots-Tag header, and a noindex meta tag applied at runtime
  (applyNoIndexHead in src/lib/seo.ts).

3.8 ANALYTICS AND CONSENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  src/lib/cookieConsent.ts — the banner's choice; when accepted it loads Google
  Analytics (id from .figma/make/site.json) with ad signals denied; when
  declined or unanswered, no Google script or request is made at all.
  src/lib/siteAnalytics.ts — FIRST-PARTY, consent-gated measurement through
  Supabase RPCs: record_site_page_view (path, locale, referring host) and
  record_site_event (download_click / purchase_click). Explicitly no IP, no
  account id, no user agent, no cookie, no visitor identifier. Failures never
  block navigation. Platform operators can purge old rows in /admin/traffic.

3.9 ACCESSIBILITY AND MOTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - a skip target (#main-content) with a single <main>
  - every icon-only control carries aria-label; menus use role="menu",
    aria-haspopup, aria-expanded, aria-controls
  - the mega menu and mobile drawer manage focus and Escape
  - useLandingMotion() reveals sections once and respects
    prefers-reduced-motion / the site config's ignoreReducedMotion flag
  - Google Analytics is never loaded before consent (see 3.8)

3.10 VISUAL SYSTEM AND STYLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  src/marketing/landing.css — everything is scoped under .landing-atlas so the
  landing cannot leak styles into the dashboard. Blue/white enterprise palette,
  self-hosted Inter-parity type scale, restrained motion. The dashboard keeps
  its own theme (src/index.css tokens: canvas/surface/line/ink/brand/warn/
  serious/good, chart colours, sidebar tokens).


================================================================================
 PART 4 — AUTHENTICATION SCREENS + FIRST-RUN ONBOARDING
================================================================================
FILES: src/components/Auth.tsx, src/modules/auth/** (forms split per screen:
SignInForm, SignUpForm, ForgotPasswordForm, shared AuthShell / FormAlert /
PasswordField), src/hooks/useSupabaseAuth.ts.

 WHY THEY HAVE URLS: the login form used to be local state, so it could not be
 linked, bookmarked or opened in a new tab. Each screen now owns a URL and the
 URL is the source of truth (src/App.tsx maps URL -> view).

 /login
   - e-mail + password, "show password" toggle, submit button with loading state
   - "forgot password" link -> /forgot-password, "create account" -> /signup
   - signing in calls supabase.auth.signInWithPassword; success is picked up by
     useSupabaseAuth, which reads the user's profile row (name, role) and calls
     store.signIn(name, email, role, userId)
   - the account's data partition is switched at that moment (see PART 6)

 /signup
   - full name, e-mail, password (+ password rules display), a checkbox linking
     to the privacy policy, and the "already have an account" link
   - account creation goes through Supabase Auth; the profile row is created by
     a database trigger (supabase/schema.sql)

 /forgot-password
   - e-mail field -> Supabase sends the reset mail; the screen explains that a
     link will be sent and never reveals whether the address exists

 /reset-password
   - shown when Supabase hands back a recovery session (App.tsx watches for it
     and forces this route); two fields (new password + confirmation), then
     back to /login

 CLIENT-SIDE GUARDS (src/modules/auth/lib/validation.ts) — friendly feedback
 only; the real enforcement is Supabase + RLS:
   e-mail shape + max 254 chars, injection signatures stripped/refused,
   password 8..72 bytes (bcrypt limit), sanitizeInput() removes control
   characters and script/SQL signatures.
 PROVIDER ERROR MAPPING (src/modules/auth/lib/authErrors.ts + Auth.tsx):
   raw GoTrue English errors are translated to friendly French (invalid
   credentials, e-mail already registered, weak password, e-mail not confirmed,
   rate limit, network) — a raw provider error is never shown.
 IDLE AUTO-LOGOUT: src/lib/useIdleLogout.ts — 20 minutes without mouse,
   keyboard or touch activity signs the user out and lands on /login with an
   explanatory message (IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS in App.tsx).
 LOCAL DEVELOPMENT MODE: src/lib/localDevMode.ts — when Supabase is not
   configured (and only in a dev build) the app can run on a local, clearly
   labelled development account; the Topbar shows a "local dev" badge so it can
   never be mistaken for a real session.
 THE "NO ACCESS" SCREEN: an account whose role/team permissions allow no module
   at all sees a dedicated screen with a sign-out button.
 UNCONFIGURED NOTICE: src/components/SupabaseSetupNotice.tsx explains which
   .env variables are missing and how to set them.

 FIRST-RUN ONBOARDING MODAL (src/components/OnboardingModal.tsx +
 src/hooks/useOnboarding.ts + src/lib/onboardingTypes.ts)
   - shown once per account, after sign-in, when the user has no row in the
     Supabase table onboarding_responses
   - questions: "how did you hear about us" (Google search, social media, word
     of mouth, online ad, blog article, other + free text) and "what kind of
     business are you" (small company, self-employed, professional,
     tradesperson, individual), plus a notification opt-in
   - deliberately FAILS OPEN: if the questionnaire cannot be read, the
     dashboard is shown rather than blocked
   - completion is recorded server-side as a response row, always scoped to the
     signed-in user

 PART 5 — THE DASHBOARD: APP SHELL AND ALL 13 MODULES
================================================================================
5.0 THE SHELL (src/App.tsx, src/components/Sidebar.tsx, Topbar.tsx, nav.tsx)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ROUTING RULES (enforced by redirect effects, never by silently swapping screen)
   - signed OUT + private URL      -> /login, remembering where you were headed
                                      so sign-in returns you there
   - signed IN + / or /login       -> first module you are allowed to open
                                      (dashboard for a full account)
   - signed IN + public page       -> still shown (e.g. /pricing stays reachable)
   - unknown path                  -> NotFound
 SIDEBAR (left navigation rail)
   - the 13 modules in fixed order, grouped: Pilotage (dashboard) /
     Opérations (projects, contacts, procurement, inventory) /
     Suivi & Échéances (calendar, risk, tools) / Conformité (legal, reports) /
     Administration (team, audit, settings)
   - collapses to an icon rail; persists its own preferences
     (localStorage key mizan.sidebar.more)
   - "Plus d'outils" collapsible row contains risk / legal / tools; team and
     audit live in the account popover
   - module SEARCH matches the module name in every UI language, so typing
     "dépenses" finds "Purchases & Expenses" even in English
   - a primary action button and quick actions (new project, new expense,
     generate contract); the invoice item is behind a disabled flag
     (INVOICE_ROUTE_ENABLED = false) because no invoicing module exists yet
   - all offsets use logical properties (ps/pe/ms/me, start/end) so the rail
     mirrors correctly in RTL
 TOPBAR
   - page title, mobile menu button, "Quick actions" menu (new project / new
     expense / generate contract -> the corresponding module), account menu
     (name, e-mail, role badge, "profile & company" -> settings, sign out),
     development-only "local dev" badge
   - menus close on outside click and on Escape
 EVERY PAGE ALSO CARRIES: LocalSaveNotice (a banner that tells the truth about
 where records are stored and whether a write failed) and a noindex head.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 /dashboard — "Tableau de bord" (src/modules/Dashboard.tsx)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PURPOSE: the money and activity overview, computed only from the account's own
 records (src/lib/dashboardData.ts).
 ON SCREEN
   - 4 summary cards, all-time totals in the account currency:
       * Budgets des projets      = sum of project budgets you typed (not
                                    invoiced revenue)
       * Encaissements saisis     = sum of the "paid" amount you typed per
                                    project (no dated payment history exists)
       * Dépenses approuvées      = sum of expenses with status approved only
       * Soldes clients à recouvrer = sum of positive client balances (money
                                    owed to you; negative balances ignored)
   - a period selector 6 / 8 / 12 months (default 8) that drives every chart
   - three toggleable widgets (a "Widgets" button opens the show/hide row):
       * Dépenses mensuelles   — area chart of approved expenses per month
       * Répartition           — donut of approved expenses by category
       * Budgets et encaissements — grouped bars, top 8 projects by budget
   - three quick-action tiles (new project / new expense / new contract) that
     navigate to the module
   - "Activité récente" feed of the last audit events with severity badges, and
     a link to the full audit log
   - honesty notes rendered in the UI: a cash note stating that "collected"
     means typed amounts, not a bank balance, and that no monthly revenue or
     cash-flow is estimated without dated payment history
 FUNCTIONS
   - doExport("csv" | "excel" | "pdf"): CSV and Excel of the monthly approved
     expense series (disabled when the period has no entries); PDF calls
     window.print(); exports run inside runAsync() which reports a failure
     instead of crashing
   - the widget toggles; the period selector
 EMPTY / DATA-QUALITY STATES
   - no records at all -> "Aucune donnée financière enregistrée" card instead
     of fake numbers
   - dashboardRecords() hides rows left over from old demo seeds and reports
     "N exemple(s) préchargé(s) exclus de cette vue"
   - invalid rows (non-finite amounts, unknown statuses, bad dates) are rejected
     and counted in an alert line
   - money is summed in integer cents to avoid floating-point drift

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 /projects — "Projets" (src/modules/Projects.tsx)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 LIST: name, client, status (planning / active / on hold / done), progress bar,
 budget, paid, cost, start and end dates.

================================================================================
 PART 6 — THE STORAGE ENGINE (src/store/useMizan.ts, defaults.ts, seed.ts)
================================================================================
 WHAT IT IS: one Zustand store persisted to localStorage, holding
   settings, projects, contacts, expenses, stock, contracts, team, tools, audit
   plus the current session (auth).
 PER-ACCOUNT PARTITIONING
   - storage key while signed out : mizan.store.v2.signed-out
   - storage key while signed in  : mizan.store.v2.<encodeURIComponent(userId)>
   - signIn() switches the storage name, resets the in-memory state to empty
     defaults and rehydrates, so a new account can never inherit another
     account's (or the demo's) records
   - signOut() keeps the account's saved records on disk but removes them from
     memory and stops writing into that account's key
 INPUT SANITISATION: every stored string passes sanitize() — angle brackets
   removed, control characters removed, trimmed, capped at 400 characters —
   before it enters state (XSS/injection defence in depth).
 WRITE-TIME VALIDATION: businessValidation.ts enforces non-negative amounts
   (MAX_AMOUNT 999 999 999 999.99), quantities (MAX_QUANTITY), a valid VAT rate
   (0..100), valid date ranges, and calendar helpers in calendarDates.ts.
 FAILED-WRITE DETECTION: localSaveStatus.ts wraps the storage engine; a write
   that fails (private mode, quota, disabled storage) surfaces in the
   LocalSaveNotice banner instead of being silently lost.
 LEGACY DATA: legacyBackup.ts + the dashboard's seed-provenance detector keep
   old demo rows out of views without deleting them, and can export a copy.
 WHAT IS **NOT** HERE: no server sync. The Supabase tables in PART 9 exist for
 auth, CMS content, subscriptions and analytics; the business records of the
 signed-in modules are browser-local in this release. The README, SECURITY.md
 and the in-app notices all state this.

    client.
  - Admin roles are rows: profiles.is_admin (content) and platform_admins
    (operations) — deliberately separate so a content editor cannot touch
    subscriptions or access rights.
  - tests/schema.test.ts loads the schema into PGlite (an in-process Postgres)
    and asserts the definitions, so the SQL is covered by the test suite.
  - docs/SUPABASE_OPERATIONS.md describes setup, permissions and migrations.

CONFIGURATION (.env.example, .env.production, src/lib/supabaseConfig.ts)
  VITE_SUPABASE_URL        https://<project-ref>.supabase.co
  VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY   the public anon/publishable key of the SAME project
  VITE_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY  optional CAPTCHA on the CMS login

  REQUIRE_SUPABASE_ENV=1 (fail a CI build that has no usable pair).
  The build inlines VITE_* values into the browser bundle, so a deployed site
  must be built with them; the app refuses to sign with a service-role key and
  reports which variable is missing. .env.production is committed with the
  project's public pair so Cloudflare Pages builds work with no dashboard

SECURITY HEADERS (public/_headers) — CSP (self + Supabase + Google Analytics +

  nosniff, HSTS, Referrer-Policy, a long Permissions-Policy allowlist,
  COOP/CORP, X-Robots-Tag noindex on /admin/*, Cache-Control immutable for
  /assets/*, per-language Content-Language.
  public/anti-reverse-engineering.js adds right-click / devtools shortcut
  blocking and a console warning — a deterrent, explicitly not a security
  control (client code is always readable by the user).

 PART 11 — HONESTY LIST: REAL vs DEMO vs STUB (read before trusting anything)
================================================================================
The repository is deliberately explicit about this. Summarised from the code,
the on-screen notices, README.md, SECURITY.md and docs/CODE_REVIEW.md:

 NOT A PRODUCTION ERP
   Business records (projects, contacts, expenses, stock, contracts, team,
   subscriptions, audit) live in THIS BROWSER's localStorage, partitioned per
   account. They are not synced to the server, not encrypted at rest, and are
   lost if the browser profile is cleared. The Supabase tables for those
   entities exist in the schema but are not what the app writes to today.

 DEMO / ILLUSTRATIVE SCREENS (contain fixed examples, no user data)
   * /calendar — a fixed September 2026 month with 7 sample entries; the page
     says so.
   * /risk — seven fixed risk rows; the page renders a demo notice.
   * /admin/monitor — counters derived from local logs; the cards explain what
     would need a server (IP logging, full analytics).
   * /admin/content-improvement — a scoring tool, not stored content.
   * /admin/contact-inbox — local messages only; "Send Draft" saves locally and
     sends nothing.
   * Marketing widgets (calendar, risk, subscription table, trend chart) are
     illustrations, and the copy labels the numbers as examples.

 CLIENT-SIDE GATES, NOT SECURITY
   * Plan gating (PlanGate / getUserPlan) is decided in the browser from
     localStorage — a product gate, not enforcement.
   * allowedModules() hides modules in the UI; with local storage as the data
     layer there is no server-side check behind it yet.
   * ProtectedRoute / PlatformAdminRoute / ContentAdminRoute exist for UX;
     their own comments say the real boundary is Postgres RLS.

 INCONSISTENCY TO KNOW ABOUT
   * /admin/site/pages writes to localStorage ("cms_pages") while every sibling
     panel in /admin/site/* uses Supabase through src/admin/site/client.ts
     (which already provides listManagedPages/saveManagedPage/deleteManagedPage
     for the cms_pages table). It is a stub that looks like the others.
   * /admin/subscription-editor edits the local "subscriptions" list — the same
     data getUserPlan() falls back to.

 NOT IMPLEMENTED / DELIBERATELY ABSENT
   * No invoicing module (the sidebar's invoice item is behind
     INVOICE_ROUTE_ENABLED = false and the button redirects to existing
     modules instead of being dead).
   * No payment provider, no webhooks: subscription status, "paid" requests and
     one-time products are all manual / click-out flows.
   * No e-mail sending from the app (support requests are stored rows; the
     contact inbox does not send).
   * No server-side IP logging or bot blocking beyond client-side rate limits.
   * No dynamic risk-scoring model — /admin/algorithms says so explicitly and
     refuses to expose invented weights.

 MEASUREMENTS ARE CLICKS, NOT CONFIRMED EVENTS
   "download_click" and "purchase_click" are recorded as clicks on a link.
   The admin dashboard and traffic screens say this in their captions; do not
   read them as completed downloads or payments.
