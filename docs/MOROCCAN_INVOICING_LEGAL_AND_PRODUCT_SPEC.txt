FATORATI.TECH — MOROCCAN INVOICING LEGAL AND PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
Research snapshot: 16 October 2026
Scope: legal research leads and product requirements, not a legal opinion. Confirm the current law and tax regime with a Moroccan accountant/lawyer before production use.

1. PRODUCT SUMMARY
Build a Moroccan-market invoice creator for freelancers, artisans and small businesses. Allow users to create and save drafts, issue invoices with a date and identifier, add buyer/seller details, enter dynamic line items and different VAT rates, calculate HT/tax/TTC, track payment status, customize the colour/theme/logo, save reusable templates, preview A4 and print/export to PDF.

Do not claim DGI certification, tax clearance, electronic-invoice transmission, or certified-accounting status. A PDF creator alone does not establish compliance.

Current implementation target:
- A first-class Invoices module under the signed-in app's Day-to-day group.
- French, English and Arabic (RTL).
- Three layouts: Standard, Modern and Friendly; custom accent colour and logo upload.
- Seller/client information and itemized lines; configurable per-line tax; totals; due date and payment tracking.
- Drafts editable/deletable; issued invoice particulars locked. Duplicate issued invoices into new drafts. Do not silently edit or erase an issued invoice.
- Browser print dialog with Save as PDF.
- Current data is browser localStorage keyed by app account identifier. No sync/cloud archive in this MVP. Sequence numbering is browser-local and may collide across devices; don't represent it as a central tax sequence.

2. MAIN MOROCCAN LEGAL SOURCES TO CHECK
This is a focused review of relevant frameworks, not “all laws”. Official sources should be treated as authoritative over blogs.

A) Code Général des Impôts (CGI), especially Article 145 and provisions on invoice particulars / ICE
Official Ministry of Economy and Finance CGI/publications:
https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/lbc/Pages/publications0.aspx
The applicable year's CGI is the source to verify invoice particulars. Depending on the taxpayer/transaction, fields can include seller identity/name, address, ICE, IF (tax ID), RC, tax/professional identifiers, date and invoice identifier, buyer identity, description, quantities, prices, taxable base, tax rate/amount, HT and TTC totals, payment terms, and special or exemption language where legally required.
Product implications:
- Support the seller’s legal name, legal form, address/city, ICE, IF, RC and RC city; TP/patente and CNSS if applicable.
- Support buyer details, line description, unit, quantity, unit price HT, tax rate, tax amount, total HT/TTC, due date, payment terms and references.
- Do not claim every field applies to every taxpayer; exact compulsory particulars depend on the current tax regime and transaction.
- VAT rates vary; rates in the UI are selection aids, not advice. 0% is not by itself an exemption reason. Let each line use its appropriate rate, including special treatment only after the user confirms it.

B) Electronic invoicing under CGI Article 145-IX and implementing rules
Official tax sources to check:
https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/lbc/Pages/publications0.aspx
Secondary commentary reviewed as of 12 October 2026 reported that the practical DGI system/timetable and implementing decree still needed official confirmation. This must be rechecked in the Bulletin Officiel and current DGI notices; do not treat a blog as authority.
Secondary context:
https://la-caisse.ma/fr/blog/facturation-electronique-maroc/
https://deadline.ma/conseils/systemes-erp-crm/facturation-electronique-dgi-maroc-2026
Product implications: no invented DGI QR, signature, clearance stamp, authorization, or transmission. Build an official integration only after official rules, technical specifications, onboarding and launch dates are published and reviewed.

C) Commercial/accounting records and retention
Official Ministry legal texts:
https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/Pages/textes-juridiques.aspx
Confirm current Code de commerce/accounting/tax retention obligations, scope, start date and exceptions with counsel/accountant. Ten-year retention is often cited for Moroccan commercial/accounting records, but do not assume one period covers every taxpayer/document.
Product implications: browser localStorage is not a statutory archive. Tell users to export and securely retain required records. A future server archive needs access control, backups, export, audit history, retention policy and legal-hold handling, not just a database table. Issued invoices should be immutable; use lawful correction/credit-note workflows as applicable.

D) Law 09-08 and CNDP
Official Law 09-08 text:
https://www.cndp.ma/images/lois/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf
CNDP formalities guidance:
https://www.cndp.ma/conditions/
Invoice data can contain personal names, phones, emails, addresses, client relationships, item details, prices and payment references.
Product implications: state purposes, collect only needed data, define retention/access/security, implement deletion and privacy-request processes, and assess CNDP notification/authorization and cross-border transfer requirements before server storage or third-party processing. Don't promise “no server data” without auditing authentication, support, logs, analytics and integrations. Local invoice storage does not mean the separate authentication service holds no personal data.

E) Law 53-05 and electronic exchanges
It forms part of the Moroccan framework for electronic exchanges and certain electronic writings/signatures, as amended by subsequent legal texts. A printed PDF is not automatically a qualified electronic signature, certified original, or proof of delivery/acceptance.
Product implications: describe exactly what the app does — renders/prints data supplied by a user. Review qualified signatures, evidence and delivery providers separately before adding them.

F) Law 31-08 on consumer protection
Relevant to Fatorati's own consumer-facing terms and commercial claims. Do not mislead users about compliance or confuse statutory rights with the platform's voluntary subscription refund guarantee. It does not certify invoices.

G) Law 69-21 on payment deadlines
Official texts index:
https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/Pages/textes-juridiques.aspx
Ministry notice:
https://www.finances.gov.ma/fr/pages/detail-actualite.aspx?fiche=7218
Payment-deadline requirements and thresholds depend on circumstances. A default 30-day term is editable convenience, not a universal rule.
Product implications: let users choose issue/due dates and terms; don't say every Moroccan invoice must be paid in a fixed time.

5. MODEL AND BUSINESS RULES
Seller fields: legal name, legal form, address, city/country, phone/email, ICE, IF, RC/RC city, TP/patente and CNSS where applicable; logo; optional payment/bank info, notes/footer.
Buyer fields: legal/person name, address, city/country, phone/email and ICE/IF/RC where applicable.
Invoice: identifier, issue date, due date, subject/reference, currency, line rows (description, unit, qty, HT unit price, VAT percentage), VAT summary by rate, HT subtotal, total tax, TTC total, amount paid, remaining balance, payment date/reference, terms and special tax text where applicable.
Calculations: line HT = quantity × unit price; calculate rounded tax by line rate; group tax by rate; HT = sum bases; TTC = HT + tax; outstanding = max(0, TTC − amount paid). Test rounding edge cases. Validate non-negative amounts, positive quantity, valid tax range, valid dates, and due date not before issue date.
Numbering: FAC-YYYY-NNNN is a UI convenience only. The current browser-local approach is not globally unique. Before multi-device/business-wide use, issue numbers using a backend transaction, tenant scoping, locking and a unique database constraint.
Payment tracking is bookkeeping metadata, not a payment processor, escrow or guarantee of customer payment. No currency conversion unless it uses a displayed rate source/date.
Template data: name, theme, colour, logo, default tax rate, terms and footer; do not carry over a previous buyer, prior payment or final invoice number.
PDF export uses browser “Save as PDF” currently; direct downloadable files and robust file names may be future improvements.

6. POLICY ADDITIONS
Terms of Service:
- User controls accuracy, required tax particulars, rates, invoice numbering and legal retention.
- Tax choices are not advice; app does not submit to DGI or certify compliance.
- Exported PDF reflects user input and is not independently verified.
- Issued particulars are locked; corrections require an appropriate separate document.
- Do not imply the app collects customer payments or guarantees settlement.
- Subscription refunds concern Fatorati purchases only, and are separate from invoices created by customers. Mandatory legal rights remain.
Privacy Policy:
- Disclose the invoice/customer fields entered, browser-local storage, access risks, no cross-device sync/remote backup in this version and user control over PDF downloads/sharing.
- Explain browser data-clearing or device loss may permanently erase these documents; Fatorati cannot recover unsynced records.
- Distinguish invoice localStorage from separately processed authentication, support, website and analytics data.
Cookie Policy:
- localStorage is browser storage, not itself an advertising cookie. Say invoices/templates may persist on the device and can be removed by clearing site/browser storage. Cookie consent isn't an invoice backup.
Refund Policy:
- Fatorati subscription refunds are distinct from users' customer invoices. Subscription guarantees cover only eligible Fatorati purchases under its refund policy; mandatory statutory rights apply.
- Support may not restore data that was never synced or backed up.
All legal page additions should be linked, translated consistently into EN/FR/AR, dated, and reviewed by a Moroccan legal/accounting professional before publication.

7. CURRENT MVP LIMITS / LAUNCH CHECKLIST
- Local browser only; not synchronized and not a legal archive.
- Local number sequence can collide on other devices; don't position as shared/team fiscal numbering.
- Browser print PDF has no qualified signature, DGI transmission, tax QR or clearance.
- User must verify rates/mandatory particulars and preserve legal records separately.
- Test multi-page A4 print, long strings, Arabic RTL, Chrome/Firefox/Safari, quantity/rounding, mixed VAT rates, duplicate/issued lock, payment edit, templates, logo validator, user isolation, corrupted/full localStorage, and legal links across locales.
- Run typecheck/lint/tests/production build in CI before merge; do not deploy until review and checks pass.
